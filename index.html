Six airports · Six different onward journeys

Know your airport before you book.

Airport geography is often overlooked. London's airports differ in terminal configuration, rail access, surrounding road networks and the practical effort of moving from arrivals to your final destination.

LHRGuide 01 / 06

Heathrow Airport

Heathrow has four active passenger terminals spread across a large airport campus. Choosing a pickup location without checking the terminal can add time to an otherwise straightforward journey.

Before booking, check your airline and terminal, allow time for immigration and baggage collection, and decide whether you want an arrivals meeting point or a simpler collection arrangement. For journeys into London, compare the cost of a direct car with rail options for the full door-to-door route, not merely the airport-to-station leg.

For a practical starting point, read the Heathrow Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. To understand example routes and what may affect quotes, check Heathrow airport taxi prices. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the Heathrow drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: An airport journey from Heathrow to a central hotel, a suburban home or a cruise connection will have different distance and vehicle requirements.
LGWGuide 02 / 06

Gatwick Airport

Gatwick has North and South Terminals with different road access and meeting arrangements. Confirming your terminal is one of the simplest ways to reduce collection confusion.

Rail can work well for travellers staying close to a suitable station. For families, luggage-heavy groups or passengers travelling beyond London, comparing the complete transfer is more useful than comparing a rail ticket to a car quote in isolation. Check the pickup instructions and whether an arrivals greeting is included.

For a practical starting point, read the Gatwick Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. To understand example routes and what may affect quotes, check Gatwick airport taxi prices. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the Gatwick drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: Journeys between Gatwick and Central London, Brighton or another airport can differ substantially in travel time and total charges.
STNGuide 03 / 06

Stansted Airport

Stansted is northeast of London, so your onward destination and landing time have a large effect on the best transport choice.

Early departures and late arrivals deserve extra attention: check train operating hours, walking distance between the terminal and onward transport, and the amount of luggage you can comfortably carry. A pre-arranged transfer can be convenient when several people travel together or a connection requires a direct route.

For a practical starting point, read the Stansted Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. To understand example routes and what may affect quotes, check Stansted airport taxi prices. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the Stansted drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: For Stansted, compare a transparent all-in quotation with the total rail or coach cost plus any final-mile transfer.
LTNGuide 04 / 06

Luton Airport

Luton Airport sits outside the centre of London and has several public-transport steps for some journeys. Think through the final destination before deciding whether the cheapest ticket is the cheapest trip.

Factor in any airport-to-station connection, walking distances, children, large bags and the possibility of changing trains. For direct transfers, the vehicle should fit both the passenger count and the luggage count; those are not always the same requirement.

For a practical starting point, read the Luton Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. To understand example routes and what may affect quotes, check Luton airport taxi prices. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the Luton drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: A low headline fare can become less compelling once transfers and last-mile travel are added.
LCYGuide 05 / 06

London City Airport

London City Airport is conveniently located for parts of East London and the Docklands, although the best onward route depends on your actual appointment or hotel address.

The DLR can be a practical choice for light luggage and straightforward connections. A road transfer may suit groups, unusually large bags or itineraries with multiple stops. Check arrivals information and account for possible road conditions around the Docklands.

For a practical starting point, read the London City Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the London City drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: Consider traffic, meeting arrangements and the final walk from a station when comparing options.
SENGuide 06 / 06

Southend Airport

Southend Airport serves a smaller terminal, but its distance from much of Greater London still matters when planning the onward trip.

Make a realistic connection plan for your final destination, especially if landing outside normal public-transport hours. For larger groups, compare the practicality of handling luggage across multiple services with a direct road journey.

For a practical starting point, read the Southend Airport Taxi service guide, which covers the relevant transfer options and booking considerations. For access to the terminal forecourt, see the Southend drop-off charge guide.

Planning note: Check the destination, service hours, baggage needs and any waiting policy when comparing the full trip.

Comparison checklist

What does the price actually include?

Ask whether waiting time, airport parking, meet-and-greet, extra stops, larger vehicles and road tolls are included. A slightly higher all-in quote can be easier to compare than a low starting price with unclear extras. For longer routes, the long-distance taxi prices in the UK guide explains additional considerations around distance, journey planning and vehicle requirements.

Beyond the terminal

Longer connections, crossings and travel decisions.

Some journeys require more than simply getting into London. Inter-airport trips, cruise departures, major venues and road crossings each introduce their own timing and transport considerations.

Between airports: Gatwick to Heathrow

Changing airports is not the same as changing terminals. Build in time for baggage reclaim, border processes, the road or rail journey and the next check-in deadline. A direct car may reduce changes, but journey time still depends on road conditions.

Review the taxi from Gatwick to Heathrow guide to understand the practical route, while checking your airline's minimum connection requirements independently.

From rail station to cruise terminal

A cruise journey may include several transport stages: airport to station, train to Southampton and final transfer to a cruise berth. Confirm the ship's terminal and embarkation deadline before selecting a route.

For the last leg, read about a taxi from Southampton train station to the cruise terminal. Passengers travelling with multiple bags should check vehicle boot capacity, not just seat count.

Silvertown Tunnel and East London roads

Journeys into the Docklands or southeast London can be influenced by the crossing used. Some routes also involve charges or time-dependent restrictions. Always check the specific journey rather than assuming every route uses the same crossing.

The Silvertown and Blackwall Tunnel charges guide explains what to review. Road-charge rules can change, so confirm current operator guidance before travel.

Choosing the cheapest useful option

The lowest advertised ticket or fare is not always the best value when a journey requires extra connections, lifts, taxis, luggage handling or overnight travel. Compare an entire route from terminal to door.

For two commonly researched journeys, examine the cheapest way from Heathrow to London and consider how to find the cheapest taxi to Luton Airport for your particular passenger and baggage needs.

Live flight information

Flight times can shift, and the airport's published arrival time is not the same as when passengers emerge with luggage. Keep the flight number available and communicate any changes to whoever is meeting you.

Travel to events and stadiums

Large venues create bursts of demand and sometimes traffic restrictions. Check event times, road closures, meeting points and realistic pickup locations in advance, particularly for groups.

The Wembley Stadium taxi guide offers event-specific planning context. For ordinary city journeys, a London taxi app comparison can help when deciding between booking methods.

Services and passenger needs

Match the transfer to the people travelling.

A comfortable journey depends on more than the number of seats. Luggage dimensions, mobility requirements, child seats, meeting procedures and stopovers can all change the most suitable arrangement.

Minibus and group journeys

A group transfer needs space for bags as well as passengers. Compare vehicle capacity, accessibility and the convenience of travelling together. The Airport Taxi Minibus guide explores group transfer options; for larger parties, review UK minibus hire prices before making a decision.

Meet and greet at arrivals

Meeting a driver inside or near the terminal can reduce confusion in an unfamiliar airport, especially for families and international arrivals. When several passengers arrive together, coordinating multiple small cars can add complexity. Check the exact terminal, number of seats, baggage room and agreed meeting point. Common questions

Answers worth checking before departure.

Is a pre-booked taxi always cheaper than the train?

No. Prices depend on distance, time, group size and last-mile connections. A rail journey may be economical for a single passenger, while direct road transport may be more practical for a group with heavy bags.

Where should I meet a driver after landing?

Use the exact collection instructions supplied by your transport provider. Airport drop-off lanes and passenger collection points may differ.

Are airport drop-off charges included in quoted prices?

Policies differ by provider. Ask for a clear all-in quote and confirm how access, parking and waiting charges are handled.

How can I compare Heathrow taxi prices?

Use the same pickup terminal, destination, travel date, vehicle size and luggage requirements for each quote, then compare what is included.

What if my incoming flight is delayed?

Check whether your provider monitors the flight and how waiting time is calculated. Share the flight number and keep communication details available.

Do I need a minibus for seven passengers?

Not necessarily the same vehicle for every group. Consider luggage volume and confirm the provider's permitted passenger and baggage capacity.

How far ahead should I plan a cruise transfer?

Allow for the cruise check-in deadline, possible traffic and the distance between your station or airport and the actual cruise terminal.
